RAGDoctor Technologies - Customer Refund & Return Policy

1. Overview
At RAGDoctor Technologies, customer satisfaction is our top priority. If you are not completely satisfied with your subscription or software license purchase, we are here to help.

2. Refund Period
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase for any monthly or annual plan. Refund requests submitted after 30 days of purchase will not be eligible for a refund, but users can cancel their subscription at any time to prevent future charges.

3. Refund Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for a refund:
- The purchase must have been made directly through the official RAGDoctor website or authorized billing portal.
- The request must be submitted within the 30-day window from the original transaction date.
- The account must not have violated our Terms of Service (e.g., API abuse or unauthorized resale).

4. Processing Time
Once a refund request is approved by our billing team, refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days. The funds will be returned to the original payment method used during checkout.

5. Exceptions
Special promotional licenses, enterprise custom contracts, and add-on API credits purchased separately are non-refundable unless specified otherwise in writing.

6. How to Request a Refund
To request a refund, please send an email to support@ragdoctor.io with your Order ID, account email address, and reason for refund.
