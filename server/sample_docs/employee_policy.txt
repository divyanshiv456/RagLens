RAGDoctor Technologies - Employee Workplace & Conduct Policy

1. Code of Conduct
All employees of RAGDoctor Technologies are expected to maintain professional standards, treat colleagues with respect, and uphold data security policies at all times.

2. Work Hours & Remote Work
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

3. Office Rules & Equipment
- Company laptops and security keys must be encrypted and protected at all times.
- Visitors must register at the reception desk before entering office facilities.
- Clean desk policy: Confidential documents must be locked away when leaving workspace.

4. Notice Period & Resignation
Employees wishing to resign from their position must provide a written 30-day notice period to their team manager and Human Resources department.
